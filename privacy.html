Privacy Policy
개인정보 처리방침

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective: May 2026

Summary: SwingForm does not sell your data. Camera access is used only for real-time swing analysis — video is processed on-device and is only uploaded to Firebase Storage if you choose to save a session. We collect only what's needed to run the app.

1Who We Are

SwingForm (스윙폼) is an AI-powered golf swing analysis app developed by Jackie Douma, operating under Sageera (ABN available on request). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SwingForm iOS app.

Contact: support@swingform.app

2Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Camera data — processed in real time on your device to detect body pose and analyse swing mechanics. Raw video is not stored unless you explicitly save a session.
  • Swing session data — scores, timestamps, tempo ratios, and kinematic results are stored locally and optionally synced to Firebase Firestore if you are signed in.
  • Account information — if you use Sign in with Apple, we receive a unique user identifier and optionally your name and email address. We do not receive your Apple ID password.
  • Purchase information — subscription status is verified via Apple's StoreKit. We do not store your payment details.
  • App analytics — anonymised crash reports and usage events via Firebase Analytics to help us fix bugs and improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included.

3How We Use Your Data

  • To analyse your golf swing and provide real-time coaching feedback
  • To store and display your session history and progress over time
  • To verify your Pro subscription status
  • To send you in-app notifications about fatigue or session milestones (no marketing emails)
  • To fix bugs and improve app performance using anonymised crash data

4Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in the following locations:

  • On-device — swing analysis runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No camera data leaves your device during analysis.
  • Firebase Firestore — session history and user profile data, stored in Google's asia-northeast3 (Seoul) region for Korean users.
  • Firebase Storage — swing videos you choose to save, stored in the same Seoul region. You can delete these at any time from within the app.

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Firebase data is encrypted at rest.

5Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with:

  • Google Firebase — for database, storage, and analytics (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • Apple — for subscription management and Sign in with Apple
  • Google AdMob — free tier users may see contextual ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers in accordance with Google's privacy policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

6Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your session data
  • Opt out of analytics by disabling tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy → Tracking

To request data deletion, email us at support@swingform.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process requests within 30 days.

7Children's Privacy

SwingForm is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via an in-app notice. Continued use of SwingForm after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

Privacy questions?

We're happy to answer any questions about how your data is handled.
